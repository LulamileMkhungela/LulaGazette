What’s inside

A clear first stop for South African legal material — educational information, kept separate from legal advice.

Acts, cases & regulations

National legislation, judgments of the Constitutional Court, SCA and High Courts, regulations, notices and provincial laws across the nine provinces.

Document reader

An in-app PDF reader with a details rail — author, year, pages and plain summaries — so documents are easier to work through.

Audience modes

Switch between Individual and Lawyer: navigation, templates and the directory follow the audience you choose.

Guides & templates

Plain step-by-step guides and draft starters tagged for individuals and/or lawyers — always review before filing anything.
